Engagement details & common questions

Everything you need to know about partnering with ClearCove to architect, deploy, and scale your AI advantage.

How are your consulting engagements structured?

We operate on a value-based model to ensure predictable ROI. Foundational blueprints and custom agent deployments are billed as flat-rate projects, while our ongoing architectural advisory is structured as a dedicated monthly retainer. You will never see a surprise hourly overage.

Will my team need technical expertise to manage these AI agents?

Not at all. We architect solutions to fit seamlessly into your existing workflows. Every deployment includes comprehensive team training, handover documentation, and a 30-day optimization period to ensure frictionless adoption by your non-technical staff.

What are your standard payment structure?

For project-based engagements, we typically structure payments to align with clear deliverables—usually 50% to initiate the architectural design phase, and the remaining 50% upon successful deployment, integration, and team handover.

How do you ensure our proprietary company data remains secure?

Security is our architectural foundation. We utilize enterprise-grade, isolated AI environments. Your proprietary data is strictly governed, never used to train public LLM models, and integrated using secure APIs that respect your internal compliance standards.

What exactly do we receive from the Strategic Blueprint engagement?

We conduct a deep-dive friction mapping of your operations. You will receive a vendor-agnostic, step-by-step architectural roadmap detailing exactly where automation will drive the highest ROI, complete with integration timelines and technology recommendations.

What is the expected timeline for a custom AI deployment?

Strategic Blueprints are typically delivered within 2 to 4 weeks. Full custom AI Agent configuration and deployment usually takes 4 to 8 weeks, depending on the complexity of your existing tech stack and specific routing requirements.
